# Study requests, corrections and removals

## Request a study

Tell us what you want to find out and who would use the answer. A sentence is enough. Name the countries or markets, the prompt language and any engines or modes you want tested. The [study request form and engine list](.github/study-requests/TEMPLATE.md) includes consumer search experiences and direct API model versions.

> Which customer service platforms do ChatGPT and Google AI Mode recommend to a global retailer with 2,000 support agents? We want to compare the US, UK and Canada and see how regional data storage requirements affect the shortlist.

Email [support@zerorank.ai](mailto:support@zerorank.ai?subject=ZeroRank%20study%20request) with your idea, or open a pull request containing the completed [study request form](.github/study-requests/TEMPLATE.md). Use email for private proposals.

## Correct a record

Email [support@zerorank.ai](mailto:support@zerorank.ai?subject=Dataset%20correction) with the study link and the question or answer ID. Tell us which field or passage needs correcting, what you believe it should say, and any source that helps us check it. We’ll review the record and reply directly.

## Request removal from a dataset

Email [support@zerorank.ai](mailto:support@zerorank.ai?subject=Dataset%20removal%20request) with the study link, the record ID if available, and what you want removed. The record ID is enough to locate personal information without repeating it in your message. Please use email for privacy requests. We’ll review the request and follow up with you directly.
